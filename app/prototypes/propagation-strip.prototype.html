What USD/MT changed here. Two rows only make sense once converted. SAFEX is flat at home (+0.03%) but +0.44% in dollars — a rand move, not a soybean move; a South African seller and a dollar buyer had opposite days. And Dalian No.2 at 723.63 USD/MT against CBOT's 424.94 is not a 70% China premium — it is the A0 food-grade bean, which is exactly bug #152. Converting to one unit made a defect visible that two currencies had hidden.

The claim this variant makes: "here is every leg, in one comparable unit, and when it last spoke." It never asserts causation and never blends board, physical, administered and weekly-assessment prices into a single headline number. Cost: eleven rows is a table, not a glance.

C

Gap board — this market against the complex

not "did it move" but "did it move like the others"

Each leg's USD session move against the median USD move of the legs that printed the same day (+0.44% here). Measuring in dollars is what makes the comparison legitimate at all — a median across CNY, ZAR and cents/bu would be arithmetic on four different things. Legs that did not print show as an explicit gap, never as 0.00%.

D

Relay — who has spoken since the event

the 6am question, answered as a single cut

One cut line: the event. Left of it, prints that predate the news and cannot contain it. Right of it, prints that could. Each step carries USD/MT over the home-currency print. Reading order is settlement order, not importance order — so the freshest leg leads even when it is not the biggest market.

The claim this variant makes: "three markets have priced this, three have not yet, three cannot." It answers the 6am question most directly and makes "has not printed" structurally impossible to confuse with "did not move." Risk: it needs an event time — and nothing in the stack produces one, because there is no news layer. Without a hand-entered cut, this variant has no spine.

·

The five constraints every variant had to obey

  • No observed timestamps. Schema stores dates only (M4 / #146). Every clock face here is a static venue schedule.
  • Silence ≠ flat. A leg that has not printed must never render as 0.00%.
  • Dark ≠ silent. India's blank is our ingest failure (#155), not the market declining to move.
  • Kinds do not mix. Board / physical / administered / weekly-assessment are four different objects; a shared % column quietly claims they are one. USD/MT makes them comparable, not equivalent.
  • The USD number carries an FX timestamp of its own. FX closes are New-York-afternoon marks, so a 07:00 UTC Dalian settle converts at a rate struck ~11h later. Same-day comparable, never simultaneous — and the strip says so out loud.
